Development process

How a project is delivered.

You always know which stage the work is in. Nothing moves into build until the scope and the quote are agreed.

  1. Step 01

    Discovery

    A working session on goals, users and constraints. You leave with a written scope.

  2. Step 02

    Quote

    A written price for the package and scope. Development starts after you accept it.

  3. Step 03

    Design

    Flows and interface, reviewed with you before engineering locks them in.

  4. Step 04

    Development

    Laravel, React, Flutter or React Native, with the APIs and accounts the product needs. You see the build as it comes together.

  5. Step 05

    Test and launch

    Testing on current browsers, and on iOS and Android for apps. Then deployment. Mobile apps include store-submission support.

  6. Step 06

    Handover

    A walkthrough of the finished website or mobile application once it is deployed.
