Development process

How a project is delivered.

You always know which stage the work is in. Nothing moves into build until the scope and the quote are agreed.

  1. Step 01

    Discovery

    A working session on goals, users and constraints. You leave with a written scope.

  2. Step 02

    Quote

    A written price for the package and scope. Development starts after you accept it.

  3. Step 03

    Design

    Flows and interface, reviewed with you before engineering locks them in.

  4. Step 04

    Development

    Laravel, React, Flutter or React Native, with the APIs and accounts the product needs. You see the build as it comes together.

  5. Step 05

    Test and launch

    Testing on current browsers, and on iOS and Android for apps. Then deployment. Mobile apps include store-submission support.

  6. Step 06

    Handover and care

    A walkthrough of the finished application. Partner or Care can continue after launch if you want the same team on call.

Questions

What does SMARTINDIA.DIGITAL build?+

Web applications and mobile applications. Web work covers custom products in Laravel and React, including dashboards, APIs and commerce flows. Mobile work covers iOS and Android, native or with Flutter and React Native.

How is the price decided?+

Pick a package. We confirm a written quote for that scope before development. Project builds are one engagement. Partner is a monthly retainer. Care is support after a mobile app has launched. The figure is part of the quote, not a rate printed in advance.

How long does delivery take?+

Websites and stores in the portfolio were delivered in 2 to 4 days. A custom web or mobile application takes the six stages above and is scheduled after the quote is accepted. You are told the plan with the quote.

What happens after launch?+

You get a handover walkthrough. Web clients can stay on the Partner retainer. Mobile clients can stay on Care. Both keep the product updated, secure and performing.
